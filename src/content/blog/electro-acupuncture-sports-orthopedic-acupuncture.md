If you're dealing with a nagging sports injury, chronic joint or nerve pain, tension from repetitive movement, or you're working your way back from an injury or surgery, standard relaxation-focused acupuncture isn't always enough. Electro-acupuncture and orthopedic/sports acupuncture are more targeted techniques designed specifically for musculoskeletal and nerve-related conditions, helping you move, train and function without pain.

At Inhale Wellness Studio, Michelle Stobart, L.Ac. combines traditional acupuncture with electro-stimulation and movement-mechanics assessment to treat the *root* of pain and injury, not just the symptoms.

## What is electro-acupuncture?

Electro-acupuncture builds on traditional acupuncture by adding a gentle electrical current to select needles once they're placed. This creates a stronger, more sustained stimulation of nerve and muscle tissue than needling alone, which makes it especially effective for:

- Chronic or stubborn pain that hasn't responded to other treatment
- Nerve-related conditions like neuropathy or nerve compression
- Muscle spasm, tightness and post-injury tension
- Post-stroke recovery and motor function support
- Reducing inflammation and speeding tissue healing

The current is precisely controlled and calibrated to your comfort level. Most patients describe it as a mild, rhythmic tapping or pulsing sensation, not a shock.

## What is sports & orthopedic acupuncture?

Sports and orthopedic acupuncture applies acupuncture through the lens of anatomy and movement. Rather than treating pain generally, Michelle assesses *how* you move, identifying the specific muscles, motor points (where a nerve enters a muscle) and movement patterns contributing to your pain or limiting your recovery.

This approach is especially effective for:

- Sports and overuse injuries (runner's knee, tennis elbow, shin splints, strains and sprains)
- Low back, neck and shoulder pain
- Sciatica and joint pain
- Post-surgical or post-injury rehabilitation
- Tendonitis and chronic tightness that limits range of motion
- Getting back to training, competition or daily activity faster, with less risk of re-injury

## Who is this for?

This treatment approach is a strong fit if you're:

- An athlete or active person dealing with an injury, overuse pain or a performance-limiting issue
- Recovering from a stroke, surgery or significant injury and want to support your rehabilitation
- Living with neuropathy, sciatica or another nerve-related condition
- Managing chronic pain that hasn't fully responded to other treatments
- Looking for a non-pharmaceutical option to reduce reliance on pain medication
